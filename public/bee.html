How to Play

Make as many words as you can from the 7 letters in the hive.

  • Words must be at least 4 letters long.
  • Every word must include the center letter.
  • Letters can be used more than once.
  • Answers follow the NYT Spelling Bee's own word list, built from years of its past puzzles: everyday words and their forms count; proper nouns, hyphenated words, and obscure Scrabble-only words don't.

Scoring

  • 4-letter words are worth 1 point.
  • Longer words earn 1 point per letter.
  • Each puzzle has at least one pangram, a word that uses all 7 letters. Pangrams are worth 7 extra points.

Your rank climbs from Beginner up to Genius (70% of the total points) and Queen Bee (every word found). Tap the rank bar to see the thresholds.

Daily gives everyone the same hive, resetting at midnight, and counts on the leaderboard. Your time to reach Genius is recorded — the clock only runs while the game is open on screen. Practice gives endless random hives.

Keyboard: type letters, Enter to submit, Backspace to delete, Space to shuffle.
