If a guess repeats a letter, only as many copies light up as actually appear in the answer — the rest show absent, same as the original Wordle.

Daily gives everyone the same word each day, resetting at midnight — play it to show up on the leaderboard. Practice is unlimited and doesn't count toward scores.

Daily games are timed from your first keystroke, and a win shows a Luck rating — how many possible words were still in play right before your winning guess. A low score means you solved it through deduction; a high score means you got there despite the odds.
