How to Play

Connect letters around the square to make words, and use all 12 letters.

Tap or click letters one at a time, or press and slide your finger (or hold the mouse button and drag) across letters to spell a word in one motion. Letters on the same side as the last one are skipped while you slide. Let go, then press Enter to submit.

  • Words must be at least 3 letters long.
  • Consecutive letters in a word can't come from the same side.
  • The last letter of each word becomes the first letter of the next.
  • Letters can be used more than once.

Try to finish in as few words as you can. The puzzle shows a par to aim for, and every puzzle can be solved in 2 words.

Delete removes a letter. Keep deleting past the start of a word to reopen the previous one. Restart clears everything.

Daily gives everyone the same box, resetting at midnight, and counts on the leaderboard: fewer words is better, and time breaks ties. The clock only runs while the game is open on screen. Practice gives endless random boxes.

Keyboard: type letters, Enter to submit a word, Backspace to delete.
